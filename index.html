Manufacture Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

Details regarding chronometer regulation, service schedules, warranty protection, and salon appointments.

What is the difference between COSC and METAS Master Chronometer testing?

COSC tests only the bare uncased movement for 15 days in laboratory conditions (-4 to +6 sec/day). METAS tests the fully cased watch in real-world conditions, requiring 0 to +5 sec/day accuracy while subjecting the watch to extreme 15,000 Gauss magnetic fields and water immersion.

How often should a ClassicCrown mechanical watch be serviced?

Due to our proprietary low-friction co-axial escapements and synthetic Moebius lubricants, we recommend a complete manufacture service every five to seven years. Annual water-resistance gasket checks are complimentary at all flagship salons.

What is covered under the ClassicCrown 8-Year Manufacture Warranty?

Our 8-year international warranty covers all mechanical calibre components, gear train alignment, escapement regulation, and factory seal integrity. If your movement experiences unexpected timing drift, our master watchmakers will regulate and service it free of charge.

How can I authenticate a pre-owned ClassicCrown timepiece?

Every timepiece features an individually engraved movement serial and case number registered in our Geneva Archival Ledger. Presenting the watch at any official salon allows our watchmakers to verify its provenance and service history within minutes.
