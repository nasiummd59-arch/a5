1. Introduction to Cookies & Digital Tracking

This Cookie Policy explains how ClassicCrown Manufacture S.A. ("ClassicCrown", "we", "us", or "our") deploys cookies, web beacons, pixel tags, and local storage mechanisms on our official website (classiccrown.com). We believe that digital privacy must mirror the precision and integrity of our mechanical watchmaking.

When you visit our digital atelier, small data files ("cookies") are placed on your computer, tablet, or mobile device. These files enable our web infrastructure to remember your device preferences, optimize interactive horological complication renderings, authenticate salon concierge submissions, and evaluate site performance.

2. Categories of Cookies Deployed Across Our Platform

We classify the cookies utilized on our platform into four transparent operational tiers:

A. Strictly Necessary Cookies (Essential)

These cookies are indispensable for the core operation of our website. They facilitate secure session authentication, private salon reservation form tokens, load balancing across Swiss server clusters, and the preservation of your cookie consent state. Disabling these cookies will impair website functionality.

B. Preference & Functionality Cookies

These cookies remember your personalized interface settings, such as your chosen language, complications filter preferences, escapement simulation audio volume settings, and display contrast configurations, delivering a seamless return visit.

C. Performance & Analytics Cookies

These cookies collect aggregated, anonymous telemetry regarding how patrons navigate our website. They measure page load speed, scroll depth across horological monographs, most viewed complication calibres, and error rates, enabling our digital architects to refine user flows.

D. Security & Anti-Fraud Cookies

These cookies help detect automated bot attacks, rapid-fire form submissions, distributed denial-of-service attempts, and protect the integrity of our Master Chronometer Registry lookup queries.

4. Google Analytics & Telemetry Governance

Our website utilizes Google Analytics 4 (Measurement Tag: G-0LY0HY7L01). We implement strict IP anonymization by default, ensuring that your complete IP address is never stored or processed on Google's servers. Telemetry data is utilized strictly in aggregate to evaluate content popularity and technical responsiveness.

You can prevent Google Analytics from collecting your browsing data across all websites by installing the official Google Analytics Opt-Out Browser Add-On (available at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).

5. Third-Party Web Technologies & External CDNs

To ensure rapid loading times and typography excellence, our website loads style assets and icon libraries from trusted external Content Delivery Networks (CDNs):

  • Google Fonts: Delivers our Cinzel, Montserrat, Italiana, and Fira Code typographic font files directly to your browser cache.
  • FontAwesome CDN: Delivers vector iconography for complications and luxury crown emblems.

These CDN requests transmit only standard browser HTTP headers and do not track individual patron profiles across the broader web.

6. How to Manage, Block & Delete Cookies

You maintain complete control over cookie storage on your devices. You can modify your browser settings to accept, block, or delete cookies at any time. Consult your browser's documentation for exact instructions:

  • Google Chrome: Settings > Privacy and Security > Cookies and other site data
  • Apple Safari: Preferences > Privacy > Manage Website Data
  • Mozilla Firefox: Options > Privacy & Security > Cookies and Site Data
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings > Cookies and site permissions > Manage and delete cookies

Please note that if you choose to disable essential cookies, certain features of our interactive complication selector and salon concierge reservation forms may not function as intended.

7. Policy Updates & Concierge Inquiries

ClassicCrown may update this Cookie Policy periodically to reflect technological advancements or statutory updates. When modifications occur, the updated policy will be published here with a renewed revision date.
