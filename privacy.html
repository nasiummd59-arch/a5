1. Preamble & Atelier Scope

ClassicCrown Manufacture S.A. ("ClassicCrown", "Manufacture", "we", "our", or "us"), headquartered at 42 Rue du Rhône, 1204 Geneva, Switzerland, is committed to safeguarding the personal privacy, confidentiality, and data autonomy of our distinguished patrons, horological collectors, prospective salon visitors, and digital guests.

This Privacy Policy delineates the exact operational frameworks, technical safeguards, and legal standards governing how ClassicCrown collects, manages, processes, stores, and protects personal data obtained through our official digital portal (classiccrown.com), our private salon reservation desks in Geneva and New York, our Master Chronometer archival vault, and during bespoke bespoke commissions.

We strictly adhere to the highest international data privacy regulations, including the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP), the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (EU GDPR 2016/679), the UK Data Protection Act 2018, the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA), and applicable global data privacy statutes.

2. Personal Information Collected Across Touchpoints

To deliver flawless haute horlogerie services, bespoke manufacture consultations, chronometer pedigree tracking, and tailored patron experiences, ClassicCrown collects several categories of personal data based on your specific interactions with our ateliers:

A. Information Voluntarily Provided by Patrons

  • Patron Identification Details: Full legal name, honorific title (e.g., Lord, Ambassador, Dr., Mr., Ms.), salutation preferences, and preferred language of correspondence.
  • Contact Information: Personal or business email address, direct telephone number, postal residence address, private salon delivery destinations, and courier verification details.
  • Private Salon Reservation Data: Desired atelier location (Geneva flagship or New York salon), appointment date, preferred time slot, requested complications for viewing (Tourbillon, Perpetual Calendar, Minute Repeater, Skeletonized Chronograph), and specialized security accommodation requests.
  • Horological Commission Specifications: Custom engraving directives, case metallurgy selections (904L surgical steel, Grade 5 aerospace titanium, 18K royal gold, or forged carbon composite), bespoke dial Guilloché patterns, strap sizing, and personal dedication messages.
  • Collector Registry & Provenance Records: Calibre serial numbers, chronometer certification registration keys, purchase date, authorized retailer documentation, and recorded ownership lineage within our private registry vault.
  • Concierge Communications: Inquiries submitted via our digital concierge desk, correspondence records, customer feedback, and bespoke horological restoration service tickets.

B. Information Collected Automatically via Digital Infrastructure

  • Technical & Network Telemetry: Internet Protocol (IP) address, approximate geographic location (country and city level), Internet Service Provider (ISP), browser family, operating system architecture, screen resolution, and hardware device profile.
  • Digital Navigation Metrics: Pages viewed, time duration spent per horological monograph, scroll depth across complication diagrams, interactive escapement frequency simulator engagements, click trajectories, and referring URL paths.
  • Session Identifiers & Analytics Data: Anonymous session tokens, Google Analytics tracking identifiers (gtag.js profile G-0LY0HY7L01), and essential user interface preference cookies (such as theme rendering and consent state).

3. Purpose of Processing & Atelier Objectives

ClassicCrown processes collected personal data exclusively for transparent, legitimate, and clearly defined horological objectives. We never sell, rent, or trade your personal data to external commercial entities. Our processing activities encompass:

  1. Executing Private Salon Appointments: Facilitating one-on-one appointments with Master Watchmakers, reserving dedicated private viewing vaults, and coordinating secure transportation or reception protocols at 42 Rue du Rhône or 645 Fifth Avenue.
  2. Managing Haute Horlogerie Commissions: Processing bespoke piece orders, tracking atelier assembly milestones in Geneva, coordinating laser and hand engraving, and delivering finished chronometers with certified provenance documentation.
  3. Maintaining Master Chronometer Pedigree: Operating the permanent ClassicCrown Registry Vault to document COSC and METAS chronometer performance certificates, warranty status, servicing history, and anti-counterfeiting micro-engraving validations.
  4. Patron Communication & Gazette Distribution: Transmitting personalized horological updates, invitations to private manufacture exhibitions, technical monographs from our Master Watchmaker Guild, and critical servicing notifications.
  5. Platform Security & Fraud Prevention: Protecting our digital architecture against unauthorized intrusions, bot automation, distributed denial of service (DDoS) vectors, and preserving the integrity of our confidential collector records.
  6. Regulatory & Legal Compliance: Fulfilling mandatory Swiss corporate record-keeping obligations, anti-money laundering compliance for high-value physical timepiece deliveries, tax reporting, and responding to lawful judicial orders.

4. Legal Bases Under GDPR & Swiss FADP

Under European and Swiss data protection jurisprudence, every data processing activity conducted by ClassicCrown rests upon an established lawful basis:

  • Performance of a Contractual Agreement (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)): Processing necessary to fulfill your appointment reservation, bespoke watch assembly, servicing contract, or deliver horological goods.
  • Compliance with Legal Obligations (GDPR Art. 6(1)(c)): Processing required by Swiss federal commercial statutes, customs regulations, tax auditing standards, and international compliance laws.
  • Legitimate Atelier Interests (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)): Processing necessary to maintain digital security, improve our interactive web application performance, safeguard our intellectual craftsmanship assets, and protect patron records against fraudulent claims.
  • Explicit Patron Consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)): Processing based on your unambiguous affirmative consent, such as subscribing to the Horological Gazette newsletter or consenting to advanced analytical tracking cookies. Consent may be revoked at any time without retroactive prejudice.

5. Master Chronometer Registry Vault & Pedigree Protection

One of the hallmark pillars of ClassicCrown is our permanent Chronometer Registry Vault. Each timepiece manufactured in our Geneva atelier receives an indelible, microscopic laser-inscribed serial key matched to an archival database record. This ledger preserves:

  • The exact COSC (Contrôle Officiel Suisse des Chronomètres) timing sheets across 5 positions and 3 temperatures.
  • The METAS Master Chronometer 15,000 Gauss magnetic exposure test results.
  • The official hand-signed certification by the Master Watchmaker who executed final casing and regulation.
  • The recorded patron ownership lineage (accessible strictly upon authenticated patron verification).

Registry data is preserved within encrypted, air-gapped Swiss data centers with multi-factor biometric access controls. Collectors may request private anonymous registry listings where only the unique cryptographic key is published without public attribution.

7. Strict Limitations on Third-Party Disclosures

ClassicCrown maintains an uncompromising policy regarding patron confidentiality. We do not sell, rent, lease, or monetize your personal details under any circumstances. Disclosures are strictly limited to the following vetted operational partners:

  • Specialized Armored Logistics Providers: Vetted high-security courier partners (e.g., Ferrari Logistics, Malca-Amit, Brinks) charged with the insured physical transit of Haute Horlogerie timepieces.
  • Certified Technical Infrastructure Vendors: Enterprise hosting providers, cloud security partners (Cloudflare), and database maintenance engineers operating under strict Data Processing Agreements (DPAs) and non-disclosure covenants.
  • Official Certification Institutes: The COSC and METAS testing laboratories for initial chronometer calibration records.
  • Judicial & Statutory Authorities: Law enforcement or judicial bodies only when mandated by binding Swiss court subpoenas or applicable federal statutes.

8. Cross-Border Data Transfers & Adequacy Protocols

ClassicCrown operates primary servers within the Swiss Confederation and the European Economic Area (EEA), both of which maintain rigorous statutory data protection standards recognized as adequate by the European Commission.

When patron data is accessed from or transferred to our North American salon at 645 Fifth Avenue, New York, or partner locations in Asia, such transfers are executed strictly pursuant to European Commission Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs), supplementary technical encryption measures, and Swiss Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC) guidelines.

9. Data Retention & Archival Schedules

We retain personal information only for the duration necessary to satisfy the specific purposes outlined in this policy, unless an extended retention period is mandated by Swiss commercial code or international archival standards:

  • Salon Appointment Records: Retained for 24 months following the scheduled appointment, after which personal contact coordinates are automatically purged unless a continuing patron relationship is established.
  • Bespoke Commission & Order Transactions: Retained for 10 years pursuant to Swiss statutory accounting and tax compliance regulations.
  • Chronometer Registry & Provenance Records: Retained in perpetuity as an essential historical pedigree archive for the lifetime of the mechanical timepiece, subject to owner anonymization upon formal request.
  • Digital Analytics Telemetry: Anonymized server logs and Google Analytics event data are automatically purged on a 14-month rolling cycle.

10. Cryptographic Security Standards

ClassicCrown employs defense-in-depth security architectures designed to match the precision of our horological movements. Our digital and physical security protocols include:

  • TLS 1.3 encryption across all web sessions with Perfect Forward Secrecy and HSTS preload enforcement.
  • AES-256 cryptographic encryption for stored registry databases, customer communications, and archival ledgers.
  • Role-based access control (RBAC) restricting patron contact information strictly to authorized private salon directors and concierge officers.
  • Continuous vulnerability scanning, automated intrusion prevention systems (IPS), and annual third-party penetration testing.
  • Air-gapped offline archival backups of Master Chronometer provenance ledgers in secure Swiss Alpine repository vaults.

11. Your Patron Privacy Rights

Under the Swiss FADP, EU GDPR, UK GDPR, and California CPRA, you possess comprehensive legal rights regarding your personal data. These rights include:

  • Right of Access (GDPR Art. 15): The right to request a formal copy of all personal information ClassicCrown holds concerning your person or registered timepieces.
  • Right to Rectification (GDPR Art. 16): The right to correct inaccurate, outdated, or incomplete personal details.
  • Right to Erasure / "Right to Be Forgotten" (GDPR Art. 17): The right to demand the deletion of your personal records, subject to statutory retention obligations and perpetual chronometer registry anonymization.
  • Right to Restriction of Processing (GDPR Art. 18): The right to limit how we process your data during dispute reviews.
  • Right to Data Portability (GDPR Art. 20): The right to receive your personal data in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable JSON or CSV format.
  • Right to Object (GDPR Art. 21): The right to object at any time to data processing conducted on the basis of legitimate interests or direct marketing.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: The right to revoke any previously granted consent without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing.

To exercise any of these rights, please submit a written request to our Data Protection Officer at concierge@classiccrown.com or by postal courier to our Geneva headquarters. We respond to all verified requests within 30 calendar days without charging administrative fees.

If we discover that personal data of a minor has been inadvertently collected without verifiable parental consent, we will immediately take decisive steps to permanently delete such information from our active databases.
