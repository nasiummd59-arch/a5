The Swiss Origin

Born in the Val-de-Travers, Perfected in Geneva

In 1888, master horologist Jean-Luc Crown founded our first chronometer atelier in the snowy valleys of the Swiss Jura. During harsh alpine winters, local farmers transformed into master artisans, filing microscopic steel pinions and tempering delicate balance springs by the glow of oil lamps.

By 1932, ClassicCrown had relocated its primary manufacture to the prestigious Rue du Rhône in Geneva, winning international timing concourses and setting observatory precision records that remain legendary among horological historians.

Horological Principles

The Four Tenets of ClassicCrown

100% In-House Calibres

Every gear wheel, pinion, mainplate, bridge, and balance wheel is designed, CNC machined, and assembled within our Geneva manufacture walls.

Hand-Anglage Standards

We forbid automated machine chamfering. Every interior and exterior angle is beveled by hand with gentian wood pegs to the Poinçon de Genève benchmark.

1000-Hour Timing Run

Before leaving the manufacture, each watch undergoes 1,000 hours of continuous multi-position timing and thermal shock testing.

Perpetual Reparability

We maintain full original blueprints and raw metallurgical tooling for every calibre ever produced since 1888, guaranteeing lifetime reparability.
